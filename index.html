03 / Engineering judgment

Fix the failure. Improve the next change.

After recovering a production data-integrity workflow, I strengthened the change process: reviewed SQL, explicit rollback plans, staged rehearsal, and post-change checks. The useful outcome was a repeatable way to make the next change safer.

I bring the same approach to open-source work: start with the invariant, test the failure path, and state where the evidence stops. I do not present short benchmark runs as production availability.
